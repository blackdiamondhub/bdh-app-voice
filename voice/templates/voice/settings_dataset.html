About the dataset
Each clip is 16 kHz mono 16-bit WAV, 2 s before the score's peak to 1 s after it: what openWakeWord trains on. “Suggested” comes from what happened next: likely true when someone then spoke and the assistant replied or acted, likely false when nobody spoke or the assistant ended it as chatter, near miss when the score stayed under the wake threshold. The download sorts clips into positive/, negative/ and unlabelled/ by your label, else the suggestion (near misses count as negative, unknown outcomes stay unlabelled), with metadata.jsonl. Up to {{ cfg.collect_max_clips }} clips are kept; the oldest unlabelled ones are deleted first.
